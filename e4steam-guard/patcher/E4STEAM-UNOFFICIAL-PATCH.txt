What the added check does (integrated "Open to LAN" worlds only)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. A guest who joins through Steam is refused if their Minecraft nickname is
   the world owner's nickname. Minecraft recognises the owner by name, so such
   a guest could otherwise act as the owner.
2. On Minecraft versions where e4steam 0.3.2 does not bind the guest's UUID to
   the SteamID (before 1.20.2 with the "modern" jars), a nickname is pinned to
   the first Steam account that uses it. A different Steam account using that
   nickname is refused. Pins are stored in
   <config>/e4steam/guest-nicknames.properties; delete a line to free a name.

The check can only refuse a login. It never admits a player the official
build would refuse, and it does nothing for non-Steam (LAN/direct) logins.

Everything else in the jar is byte-for-byte the content of the official jar.
